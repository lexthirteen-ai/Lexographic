## POST

Eight agents run my one-person practice. Not one of them can send, book, post, pay or schedule anything.

That constraint is the whole design. Here's the setup (steal it):

🐉 **DRACO, the Chief of Staff.** It reads every other star's work and writes me one page before seven.
↳ 300 words: the one thing, what rolled over, today's hat, the shape of the day, what needs me, pipeline and money, and what not to do
↳ The one thing has to come from a plan I already ticked, so it can't invent a priority overnight
↳ It does none of the work itself. That is the job, not a limitation

⭐ **CARINA, the Managing Editor.** It plans the week and shows the arithmetic.
↳ Monday morning you get the week's goal, one thing for each working day, and what's cut
↳ Everything scored against that goal, sums written out, zero for anything the files can't back
↳ Nothing in the plan counts until you tick the box

🕐 **ALCYONE, the Scheduler.** It gives the day a shape and books nothing.
↳ Heavy, normal or open, with the count that decided it
↳ For every call: who, why, and what you already promised them
↳ On Monday it proposes your time blocks, each one a box you tick before you book it yourself

🌅 **AURORA, the Reporter.** It reads the inbox at 06:30 so you don't open it first thing.
↳ Who is waiting on whom, and for how long. Every item carries who owes the next move and how many days it has waited
↳ Leads never drop out quietly, because that's where founders lose money
↳ It never replies

✉️ **ELECTRA, the Correspondent.** It drafts every reply you owe and every follow-up you're owed.
↳ One file each, with a line of context on top
↳ It leaves the opening line for you, every time
↳ It never sends

🌙 **VESPER, the Copy Desk.** It's the gate. Nothing reaches you without going through it.
↳ Checks the item line, the character count, the marker, and the provenance line
↳ A pass goes to staged. A fail goes back to the desk that wrote it with the failing rule named
↳ It never rewrites a word. It signs off "Vesper. Staged, not sent."

🔭 **MIRA, the Ombudsman.** It holds you to the plan, with a count and no lecture.
↳ Every evening: what got done, what slipped, and how many times
↳ On Friday it lists anything that rolled over three times or more as avoided
↳ A handoff that isn't on the map gets reported

📁 **SELENE, the Archivist.** It keeps score so things stop falling through.
↳ Applies the proposals you tick, and logs each one with the date
↳ Keeps the ledger of promises: what you promised, to whom, by when, done or dropped
↳ Written on your word only, never on its own

Here's an example run, start to finish:

1. You drop in a calendar export and write your list.
2. Alcyone reads your hours and says what kind of day this is.
3. Aurora files who's waiting on you, and for how long.
4. Electra drafts the replies, first line left blank for you.
5. Vesper checks each draft and stages or rejects it, naming the rule.
6. Draco turns all of it into one page before seven.
7. You tick the boxes and carry the staged work across yourself.
8. Mira counts what actually happened that evening.

And the part I'd steal first if I were you:

**SENT is not on the map.** The pipeline ends at STAGED. One job per star, one human gate, and a box only you tick.

Trust isn't the reason. That last step is the only place left where I can still catch something, and I would rather keep it than save the twenty seconds.

All eight ship free as charters you can read and edit, with the morning brief working out of the box. You need Obsidian and about ten minutes.

Links in the comments.

If you're running your own content, inbox, planning and ops out of one head, take the whole thing.

What's the last step you still do yourself?
