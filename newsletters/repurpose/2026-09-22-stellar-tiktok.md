# TikTok Script: Six agents ran my business overnight

Source: Stellar Agents launch. Framework: emotion-to-action, steps 3 to 5 compressed per
the short-form rule. Target: 60 seconds.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)

"Six agents ran my business last night while I was asleep, and not one of them is allowed to hit send."

## Full script (estimated 60s)

**Hook:** [Direct to camera, start mid-thought, no intro] "Six agents ran my business last night while I was asleep. Not one of them is allowed to hit send."

**Pain:** "If you run your own thing, you know the 11pm version of this. The drafts aren't written, the inbox has been open in a tab since Tuesday, and the planning happens in your head on the drive home. So you start looking at automating it, and every tool out there is built to take the whole job."

**Tease and amplify:** "That's the part I'd push back on. Because the moment you automate the last step, the first thing that goes out wrong goes out under your name, at scale, while you're asleep. You don't find out until someone tells you."

**Reframe and solve:** "So I built six agents that stop one step early. Carina plans the week and routes it. Aurora reads the inbox. Lyra drafts, and leaves the first line blank on purpose, because the opening has to sound like me. Vesper checks the draft and marks it staged or rejected, naming the rule that failed. Selene keeps the record, Mira audits the other five. They run in the cloud, so my laptop doesn't have to be open. And every single one of them stops at staged."

**Takeaway:** "Staged means a human can now carry it. It does not mean sent. That last step is the only place left where I can still catch something, and I'd rather keep it than save the twenty seconds."

**CTA:** "They're free. I write about how I built them in Artificially Designed, link in bio."

## B-roll / overlay

- 0-2s. Talking head only, no overlay. The line has to land cold. **Essential.**
- 12-22s. Screen recording of the staged folder filling up overnight, or a timestamp on a file. Proof beats claim here. **Essential once the recorded footage is available.**
- 25-45s. Text overlay naming each agent as it's spoken, one at a time, lower third. Six quick cuts. **Essential.** This is the part people screenshot.
- 45-55s. Overlay the word STAGED, large, held. **Nice to have.**
- 55-60s. Talking head, no overlay.
