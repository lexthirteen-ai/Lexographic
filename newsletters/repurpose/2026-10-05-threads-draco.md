# Threads: one page before coffee

Source: Stellar Agents 0.1.3. Casual micro-observation register.

---

## Post (lead with this one)

every morning something writes me 300 words before I'm up

the one thing. what rolled over. what kind of day it is. one thing to leave alone

it does none of the work and it cannot send anything. that's the entire design

---

## Alternate, the admission

the section I didn't expect to need was "don't do today"

a short list of good ideas, written down as a no, so I stop having the same argument with myself at 4pm

turns out I wasn't short on ideas. I was short on a place to put the ones I'd already rejected

---

## Alternate, the rollover

after three days of the same item rolling over, my morning brief stops being polite about it

"tick it, or tell me it's a no"

I have told it no more times than I'd like to admit and the list has never been shorter
