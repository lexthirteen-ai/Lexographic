# TikTok Script: One page before coffee

Source: Stellar Agents 0.1.3. Framework: emotion-to-action, steps 3 to 5 compressed.
Target: 60 seconds.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)

"Every morning my chief of staff hands me one page, and it has never once sent anything."

## Full script (estimated 60s)

**Hook:** [Direct to camera, no intro, start mid-thought] "Every morning my chief of staff hands me one page. It has never once sent anything."

**Pain:** "If you run your own thing, you know how the day actually starts. You open the laptop and whoever emailed last night picks your morning for you. By eleven you've done six useful things and not one of them was the thing."

**Tease and amplify:** "I tried fixing that with a to-do list for about four years. The list just got longer, and the same item kept moving to tomorrow, and after a while I stopped seeing it."

**Reframe and solve:** "So now something else reads the calendar and the list before I'm up, and writes me 300 words. The one thing, and it has to come from a plan I already ticked. What rolled over, and after three days it asks me to tick it or admit it's a no. What kind of day it is. What I already promised the people I'm talking to. And a don't do today list, so I stop relitigating the same good idea at four in the afternoon."

**Takeaway:** "It does none of the work. It can't send, book, post, pay or schedule anything. Its entire job is to make the day small enough to hold before I've had coffee."

**CTA:** "It's free, you need Obsidian and ten minutes, and I write about how I built it in Artificially Designed. Link in bio."

## B-roll / overlay

- 0-2s. Talking head only. Let the line land. **Essential.**
- 8-18s. Screen recording of an inbox or a long to-do list, scrolling. Shows the pain without saying it. **Nice to have.**
- 22-30s. The real daily note open in Obsidian, the brief visible, boxes unticked. This is the proof shot. **Essential.**
- 30-48s. Text overlay naming each section as spoken: THE ONE THING, ROLLED OVER, TODAY'S HAT, DON'T DO TODAY. **Essential.** This is the screenshot.
- 48-60s. Talking head, no overlay.
